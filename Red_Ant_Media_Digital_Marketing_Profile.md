## 5. Technical Implementation When Required

Digital marketing remains Red Ant Media's primary focus. However, a client's positioning or growth strategy may require technical work to be implemented.

When that happens, Red Ant Media can take responsibility for the relevant implementation as part of the broader engagement.

### Examples of supporting implementation

Depending on the client's requirements, this may include:

- Business websites.
- Campaign landing pages.
- E-commerce experiences.
- Website updates and improvements.
- UX/UI implementation.
- Web or mobile applications.
- API and third-party integrations.
- Other custom digital experiences required to support the agreed strategy.

### How this works

1. **Start with the business objective:** Understand what the client wants to achieve and how the brand should be positioned.
2. **Identify the implementation requirement:** Determine whether the strategy needs a website, landing page, application, integration or another technical component.
3. **Define the scope:** Agree on the required functionality, deliverables, timeline and responsibilities.
4. **Implement:** Deliver the technical work needed to support the agreed positioning and marketing objectives.
5. **Connect it to marketing:** Ensure the implementation supports the client's digital presence, campaigns and customer journey.

**Our principle:** We do not lead with technology for its own sake. We lead with the business and marketing objective, then implement the technology required to support it.

Technical implementation is therefore a supporting, needs-based capability—not a separate primary positioning for Red Ant Media.

## 6. How We Work

We follow a collaborative process that connects business understanding, strategy, creative execution, campaign delivery and optimisation.

### Our process

| Stage | Focus | Activities |
|---|---|---|
| **01 — Discovery** | Understand the business | Requirements, research, current digital presence, audience and market review |
| **02 — Strategy** | Decide the direction | Positioning, objectives, channel selection, messaging and campaign planning |
| **03 — Creative and planning** | Prepare the communication | Content planning, creative direction, campaign assets and implementation planning |
| **04 — Execution** | Put the strategy into action | Marketing campaigns, content publishing, advertising and any agreed technical implementation |
| **05 — Measurement** | Understand performance | Analytics, campaign review, feedback and evaluation |
| **06 — Optimisation** | Improve outcomes | Refinement of content, campaigns, channels and supporting digital experiences |

### Engagement principles

- Understand the client's business before recommending activity.
- Align the work with the agreed positioning and objectives.
- Maintain clear communication.
- Coordinate creative, marketing and technical work where required.
- Review performance and identify improvements.
- Build for an ongoing relationship rather than a one-time delivery.
